## NO-CITATION

- **T-CFG-032.** No primary source in the allowed set shows that `Max-Age` makes a cookie persistent. RFC 6265 is
  cited only for §4.2.2 (REG:81). The source statements (I/08:143-147, I/24:544) give no reference. Std Questions Q10
  and Std §3.5 are cited for the rest.
- **T-AUD-011.** The claim that Logback's `totalSizeCap` arithmetic is unreliable rests on unnamed field reports
  (I/13:741-744, I/21:510, REG:200). No Logback documentation, version or issue is cited. The silent-deletion argument
  and Std §3.3/§3.4 are cited.
- **T-OBS-005.** That Tomcat meters are not instrumented unless `server.tomcat.mbeanregistry.enabled` is true
  appears only in I/21:385-386, with no Spring Boot or Micrometer reference. The research assets do not verify it.
  The backoff reason is cited to NIST SP 800-63B-4 §3.2.2 via R-RL-005.
- **T-ARCH-005.** The exclusion of Actuator, functional and servlet-registered routes from `getHandlerMethods()` is
  explicitly unverified (REG:311, I/26:450-452). The sentence now says so rather than asserting it.

## Substance changes from routing §7

- **T-ARCH-005.** Routing said coverage runs "across three registries, because `getHandlerMethods()` alone misses"
  routes. That conflates the three disposition registries with handler registries, and it asserts an exclusion that
  was never verified. Rewritten to what the test does (REG:311, I/26:443-452).
- **T-FE-017.** Routing's "only after idle expiry or eviction" reads as permitting a new session in those cases. The
  source says the opposite: those two are the only paths where a retry *would* mint one. Rewritten (I/14:234-243).
- **T-AUD-025.** Routing's reason ("an investigation cannot reconstruct a targeted attack without it") is not the
  cited ground. The primary ground is Std §3.4's mandate plus its carve-out (I/13:326-336, REG:197). The investigation
  point is kept as secondary.
- **T-ADM-022.** "Squatting" and the example `root` have no source. They are replaced with ADR-047's "targeting a
  predictable admin username" and its example `administrator`.
- **T-CFG-024.** "Matchers and handler mappings disagree" is replaced with the CVE's recorded mechanism: the filter
  chain applied no security.
