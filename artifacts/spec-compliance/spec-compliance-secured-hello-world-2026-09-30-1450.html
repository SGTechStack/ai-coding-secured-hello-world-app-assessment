Every other control that previously required a dedicated ticket (ac-6 forced password change, lm-16 metrics hook, as-11 idle/absolute session timeout, as-8 externalised secrets) was folded into an acceptance criterion on one of the 5 consolidated tickets and verified present there — see the table below.
