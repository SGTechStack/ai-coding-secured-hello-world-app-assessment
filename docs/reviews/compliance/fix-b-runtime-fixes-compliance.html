High-Priority Issues

No check reported a Critical or High finding. The most significant Medium findings, several of which multiple checks raised independently, are these:

  • Medium: race between deleting a lapsed pending registration and activating it. backend/src/main/java/sg/securedhello/registration/Registration.java (holdUsername) reads the account with the non-locking findByUsername and then deletes it, so an account activated concurrently can be deleted. Raised by springboot S-1 and code-review L-S1.
  • Medium: two concurrent registrations of one username from different addresses hit ux_username_holds_username. The resulting unmapped DataIntegrityViolationException returns 500 instead of 400 USERNAME_UNAVAILABLE. Raised by springboot S-2, code-review L-S2, im8 as-13 and semgrep (informational).
  • Medium: lm-4 audit gap. The lapsed-pending deletion in Registration.java writes no audit row, and ADR-032:67 claims a registration audit row that does not exist. Raised by im8.
  • Medium: two parallel expiry models for pending usernames (user_accounts.created_at and username_holds.expires_at). Raised by thermo-nuclear M1.
  • Medium: LockoutLadder.java accepts a consecutiveThreshold that is not a multiple of threshold. The startup floor still holds. Raised by thermo-nuclear M2.
